How This Works

Welcome to the Unresolved Childhood Trauma Simulator!

You're navigating a short, cumulative bad day. Three stats track how you're holding up — watch the bars up top; each one's caption explains what happens if it runs out.

Each turn presents a scenario with a few responses. Pick one. Your stats move — not always the way you'd expect — and the day continues. Survive the full run and you'll get an ending built from wherever your stats landed.

Every so often, a fourth option shows up with no hint attached. That one's a real gamble.

Survive once, and a harder mode — Extended Therapy — unlocks permanently: more turns, bigger swings, no mercy.

The Field Log tab is a separate, real one — for tracking actual moments in your actual life, not the fictional ones. It has its own note explaining what it is and isn't.

Everything else, you'll have to find out by playing.
